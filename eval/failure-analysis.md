# Failure Analysis

## Objective

Document situations where the system produced incorrect, incomplete, or unexpected results and analyze the likely causes.

---

## Issue 1 – Resume Fit Score Variation

### Observation

The generated resume fit score was reasonable but subjective. Running the advisor multiple times may produce slightly different scores while keeping similar strengths and recommendations.

### Likely Cause

The fit score is generated by an LLM rather than a deterministic scoring algorithm. Small variations in the model's reasoning can change the numerical score without significantly affecting the overall assessment.

### Impact

Low.

The recommendations and overall assessment remained consistent even when the exact score could vary.

---

## Issue 2 – Company Website Availability

### Observation

Some job postings did not include an official company website.

As a result, the WHOIS lookup could not provide additional legitimacy information.

### Likely Cause

The system only extracts information explicitly available in the job posting and does not attempt to infer or search for missing company websites.

### Impact

Medium.

The legitimacy assessment relied primarily on the job posting and Tavily web search instead of WHOIS data.

---

## Issue 3 – Skill Categorization

### Observation

Some technologies were grouped together or summarized instead of being listed individually.

For example, multiple testing technologies or related frameworks were combined into broader categories.

### Likely Cause

The LLM summarizes semantically similar information instead of reproducing every keyword exactly as written in the job posting.

### Impact

Low.

The extracted information remained useful for market analysis and gap identification, although a few individual technologies may not appear separately.

---

## Overall Reliability

The system produced structured outputs consistently after implementation was completed.

Most inaccuracies were related to LLM interpretation rather than software defects.

The generated reports are useful for assisting job applications, but users should review the recommendations before making final application decisions.

Critical information such as company legitimacy, fit score, and resume suggestions should be considered decision-support rather than absolute conclusions.
